About News Enhancer

News Enhancer is a web application designed to help readers better understand and contextualize news articles. Using natural language processing techniques, it analyzes articles to detect potential bias, provides concise summaries, offers contextual information, and highlights key entities.

Our goal is to promote media literacy and critical thinking by giving readers tools to analyze the content they consume more effectively.

How It Works

Article Analysis

Analyzes language patterns to detect potential bias or misleading content.

Summarization

Creates concise summaries of varying lengths to quickly grasp the main points.

Context

Provides background information about the main topics to enhance understanding.

Entity Recognition

Identifies and explains key people, places, and organizations mentioned in the article.
